# Example: "I want to learn guitar from zero, 5 hours a week"

**Assumptions:** complete beginner, acoustic guitar, 5 hrs/week, no deadline.
**Duration:** about 6 months to play full songs with chords and simple solos.

## Big picture
Setup and first chords (weeks 1-3) → Rhythm and chord changes (4-10) → First songs (11-16) → Scales and melody (17-22) → Play for people (23-26)

## Stage 1: Setup and first chords (weeks 1-3)
- **Objective:** hold the guitar comfortably, tune it, and play 3 open chords cleanly.
- **Learn:** parts of the guitar, tuning, Em, Am, E, finger placement.
- **Practice:** 15 minutes a day, 5 days a week, beats one long weekend session.
- **Milestone:** switch between Em and Am 10 times in a row without stopping.
- **Common mistake:** pressing too hard. Light, close to the fret is enough.

*(The skill continues with every stage, the first 7 days, tracking, and next steps.)*
